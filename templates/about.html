About PracticalHub

PracticalHub is a student-friendly platform that makes practical files easy to access. Students can explore branch-wise and semester-wise subjects and access individual practicals using QR codes.

In this software Faculty can upload the updated practical files & Students can scan these QR codes to access the practical files directly on their devices.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to save students' time and make practical learning simple, organized, and convenient.
